# Learning-rate schedule, epoch budget and grid reduction (decision 0016 items 5, 6, 9)

Source: `docs/small-set-gate.md` (lastfm-w3, validation only, 3 seeds, generated by `scripts/15_small_set_gate.py`).
Nothing here is a result: the small set only chooses protocol options.

## Learning-rate schedule (item 5): `none`

Best grid point of each model, mean validation Recall@20 over 3 seeds (std in brackets):

| model | none | cosine | difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| BPR-MF | 0.0447 (0.0015) | 0.0422 (0.0050) | cosine lower |
| KGAT | 0.0630 (0.0021) | 0.0638 (0.0017) | +0.0008, inside one std |
| KGAT without KG | 0.0578 (0.0056) | 0.0582 (0.0049) | +0.0004, inside one std |
| KGAT pruned | 0.0629 (0.0012) | 0.0618 (0.0010) | cosine lower |

Cosine is not better by more than the seed noise for any model and worse for two. It also needs a known epoch count at
refit time, which the median best epoch of item 4 only gives approximately. **Schedule: none** (constant learning rate), for
all models. `lr_schedule` stays at its default in the official configs.

## Learning rate 1e-4 grid points (item 9): dropped

At lr 1e-4 the validation Recall@20 is 0.031 to 0.038 for the KGAT family and 0.006 to 0.014 for BPR-MF, against about 0.06
and 0.043 at lr 1e-3. No lr 1e-4 point was the best point of any model and schedule, and they were the runs that
ran longest (about 22 of 38 hours on `lastfm-u25`). **Dropped from every grid**: KGAT and KGAT without KG keep 2 points
(`lr 1e-3`, `l2` in {1e-5, 1e-4}); BPR-MF keeps its 3 points (all lr 1e-3).

## Epoch budget (item 6): 150, patience 10 evaluations, evaluation every 5 epochs

Share of the best validation Recall@20 reached by epoch 100 / 150, at the grid points that remain, schedule none
(median over the 3 seeds, minimum in brackets):

| model, point | by 100 | by 150 |
|---|---|---|
| BPR-MF, l2 1e-4 (dim 64) | 1.000 (0.934) | 1.000 (1.000) |
| **BPR-MF, l2 1e-3 (dim 64), the selected point** | **0.941 (0.870)** | 1.000 (0.996) |
| BPR-MF, dim 32 | 1.000 (1.000) | 1.000 (1.000) |
| KGAT, l2 1e-5 (selected) | 1.000 (0.989) | 1.000 (1.000) |
| KGAT, l2 1e-4 | 1.000 (1.000) | 1.000 (1.000) |
| KGAT without KG, l2 1e-5 (selected) | 1.000 (1.000) | 1.000 (1.000) |
| KGAT without KG, l2 1e-4 | 1.000 (0.994) | 1.000 (1.000) |

`scripts/15_small_set_gate.py` pools the runs of each model (all remaining points) and gives 100 for the three KGAT-family
models, but also for BPR-MF if it is included. Read per grid point, as the rule of item 6 is meant (the point that
will be scored is the one that counts), BPR-MF at its selected point reaches only 94% of its best by epoch 100, below the 98%
of the rule, and 100% by 150. The median best epoch of BPR-MF is 120 and that of KGAT 60 to 70.

Decision: **`max_epochs = 150` for every model**, `patience = 10` evaluations (50 epochs) for every model including BPR-MF
(the small-set BPR-MF runs used 5), `eval_every = 5`. The cost is small: KGAT stops by patience long before 150 in most runs, so the cap
rarely binds; it only gives BPR-MF the room it needs. This differs from the number `docs/small-set-gate.md` prints
(100); that table is the pooled computation and is not changed by hand.
